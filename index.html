Z-Score Analysis

Statistical significance of recent performance deviation from the mean

How it works: Z-Score measures how many standard deviations recent form is from the seasonal mean. Unlike simple percentage deviation, it accounts for team consistency. A team with high variance may frequently deviate by 20%, but a Z-score of -2.0 means that deviation is statistically significant regardless of variance. Thresholds: < -1.5 = Strong Underperformance (regression UP likely), -1.5 to -1.0 = Moderate Under, -1.0 to +1.0 = Normal, +1.0 to +1.5 = Moderate Over, > +1.5 = Strong Overperformance (regression DOWN likely).
